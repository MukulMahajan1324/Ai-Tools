Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to express my enthusiastic interest in the Senior Product Designer (AI Systems) position at Google. Completing my Master of Design (M.Des) and Bachelor of Design (B.Des) at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay, my work bridges rigorous human-centered methodologies with software engineering fluency to craft scalable, intuitive product interfaces. Having followed Google's vanguard work in multimodal generative models and intuitive workspace canvases, I am eager to contribute my expertise in design systems, interaction architectures, and rapid code prototyping to your design initiatives.

With deep hands-on mastery in Figma, component architectures, and rapid prototyping, I speak the technical language of engineering partners while remaining an uncompromising advocate for the user's cognitive ergonomics.
